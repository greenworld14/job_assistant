Saved answers

          
Default answers to screening questions. They're filled in only when a new question clearly matches; anything uncertain is left for you. In text answers, {company} and {title} are replaced with the job's company and title.

        

        

          
          
        

      

      
      

    


    

      
✨ AI assistant (DeepSeek)

      
Answers screening questions that have no saved answer, using your profile. Every AI answer is marked for you to verify, and you still submit yourself.

      
When turned on, the question text, its options, the job title/company, your work history, skills, education, location and saved answers are sent to api.deepseek.com. Your email, phone, street address and resume file are never sent. AI never answers salary, consent or voluntary-disclosure questions.
